## 3. No cookies, no third-party content

This site uses no cookies. It loads no external resources (no external fonts, scripts or videos); everything is served from this site. The only connection to another service is the audience measurement (section 4). The site search runs entirely in your browser; nothing you type into it is sent anywhere. It only remembers your search term and the selected view in your browser's storage.

## 4. Audience measurement with Umami

To see how often this site is visited, which pages are read and from which websites visitors come, we use the analytics service Umami Cloud of Umami Software, Inc., San Francisco, USA.

With every page view your browser sends the following to Umami: the address and title of the page, the website you came from (referrer), screen size and browser language and – for technical reasons – your IP address and your browser's identification (user agent). Umami derives browser, operating system, device type and country from this. The IP address is not stored. To count visits, Umami creates an irreversible identifier from the IP address, the user agent and a regularly changing random value.

The measurement sets no cookies and stores or reads no data in your browser. The measuring code does not come from Umami; it is served by this site itself. If "Do Not Track" or "Global Privacy Control" is enabled in your browser, nothing is measured.

The data is stored in the EU and deleted after six months. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is to measure the reach of this site and to see which content is used. We have a data processing agreement with Umami (Art. 28 GDPR). As Umami Software, Inc. is based in the USA, access from the USA cannot be ruled out; transfers are safeguarded by the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR). The subprocessors used by Umami are listed at [umami.is/subprocessors](https://umami.is/subprocessors); for details see [Umami's privacy policy](https://umami.is/privacy). You can object to the processing at any time (Art. 21 GDPR), for example by e-mail.

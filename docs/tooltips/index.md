# ToolTips (beta)

<AddonHeader id="tooltips" />

## What it does

**Pardona's ToolTips** makes the game's tooltips more useful and easier to read – without
replacing them. Blizzard's lines stay where they are; the addon recolors some of them and adds
its own lines at the bottom.

- **Look:** position, scale, font and font sizes, and a choice of **Color set**
  (**Blizzard**, **Dark**, **High contrast**, **Pardona**).
- **Players & Mobs:** class colors, target, speed, health text on the health bar, item level of
  other players and the classification of mobs (**(Elite)**, **(Rare)**, **(Rare Elite)**,
  **(Boss)**).
- **Items:** item level, an always visible gear comparison and an option to hide the appearance
  collection hint.
- **Prices & Disenchanting:** what an item disenchants into and – with TradeSkillMaster – its
  disenchant value and whether to sell it to a vendor, put it in the auction house or
  disenchant it.

## Status

ToolTips is in **beta**. Everything described here works; features marked **(beta)** are
still being tested and can be switched off (see [Features](./features#experimental-features)).

Made for **WoW Forever**. ToolTips works on its own and fits in with the other Pardona addons:
one minimap button, one settings group **Pardona's Addons**, one look.

# Modules

::: warning
Pardona's UI is in development and not released yet.
:::

## Minimap (square)

- The minimap becomes **square**. The round ring is hidden; the map gets a fine gold frame with a purple border around it.
- The zone line above the map gets a thin frame, the zone name is shown in gold.
- The small buttons at the minimap – tracking, mail, calendar and the addon compartment (the Addons button at the minimap) – get small Pardona button frames. The clock uses the Pardona font.
- The zoom buttons and buttons of other addons stay as they are. Minimap buttons that follow the minimap shape (for example LibDBIcon buttons and the Pardona button) move to the square edge.
- Switching the module off brings back the round minimap with its ring.

## Buffs & debuffs

- Every buff and debuff icon gets a fine square border; the icons are cropped slightly.
- **Buffs** get a gold border. **Debuffs** keep the colour of their type, e.g. green for poison, purple for curses.
- The remaining time below each icon uses the Pardona font.
- Cancelling a buff with right-click works as before.

## Other interface addons

Pardona's UI does not detect other interface, minimap or loot addons. If another addon also restyles or replaces one of these parts, switch the matching module off in the settings.

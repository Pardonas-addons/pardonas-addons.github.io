# Buff Reminder

<AddonHeader id="buffReminder" />

## What it does
Pardona's Buff Reminder shows every buff you are missing in one small list on your screen – class buffs, food, flasks, weapon buffs and more. **Each reminder is a button: one click rebuffs you.** Made for WoW Forever.

- Missing buffs appear about a second after they drop, with an optional sound and chat line.
- Left-click casts the buff (highest rank you know) or uses the best item from your bags. Right-click lets you choose an item or variant, or mute the reminder.
- One keybinding handles the most urgent reminder.
- Optional: warnings before a buff expires, group buffs for your party or raid, reagents and supplies, pet reminders for Hunters and Warlocks.

## Who it is for
Every class. Classes with many self-buffs (Priest, Mage, Paladin, Warlock, Shaman, Hunter) get the most out of it; every class benefits from the food, flask, scroll and supply reminders.

## Versions
- **Stable:** 1.4.1
- **Beta:** 1.5.0-beta4 – new: pet reminders, supplies with your own items and one-click restocking at vendors, poison warnings by charges, several trackings at once, size and opacity, a smarter keybinding.

Features marked **(beta)** are experimental: on in beta builds, off by default in releases. You can switch them on in the settings at any time.

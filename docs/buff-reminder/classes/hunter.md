# Hunter

<ClassHeader cls="hunter" />

Hunters get the most out of the Buff Reminder: your aspect, your pet and your ammunition are all covered – and running out of arrows in the middle of a fight is a thing of the past.

Features marked <Badge type="warning" text="beta" /> are experimental: on in beta versions, off by default in releases – see [Versions](../#versions).

## At a glance
| Reminder | Shows when | Left-click | Tab |
|---|---|---|---|
| Aspect | no aspect is active | casts the aspect you used last | **Class Buffs** |
| Trueshot Aura | you don't have it (talent) | casts it | **Class Buffs** |
| **Pet missing** <Badge type="warning" text="beta" /> | no pet is out | Call Pet | **Pet** |
| **Pet dead** <Badge type="warning" text="beta" /> | your pet is dead | Revive Pet | **Pet** |
| **Feed pet** <Badge type="warning" text="beta" /> | your pet is content or unhappy | feeds the best food it eats | **Pet** |
| **Ammunition** <Badge type="warning" text="beta" /> | fewer than **Min.** of your equipped ammo | outside of towns: remind me in the next town; at a vendor: restock | **Supplies** |

## Class Buffs
- **Aspect** covers every aspect you know (Hawk, Monkey, Cheetah, Pack, Wild and more). Left-click casts the one you used last; with the **Variant menu on right-click** (beta) you pick another one.
- Trueshot Aura is a talent and only shows up once you have learned it.

## Pet <Badge type="warning" text="beta" />
- **Pet missing:** left-click calls your pet. Right-click offers Call Pet and Revive Pet.
- **Pet dead:** left-click revives it.
- **Feed pet:** appears when your pet is content or unhappy; with **Only when unhappy** only when it is unhappy. Left-click feeds the best food your pet accepts. Right-click shows **Pet food in your bags** – only food your pet really eats – with ★ favorites. No reminder while your pet is eating, in combat or while you are mounted.
- **No pet food:** with **Warn when no pet food** you are told when nothing in your bags fits.

## Supplies <Badge type="warning" text="beta" />
| Supply | Counts | Min. | Critical | Refill to |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ammunition** | the arrows or bullets you have equipped | 200 | 50 | 1000 |
| **Pet food** (off by default) | everything your pet can eat | 20 | – | – |

- **No ammo equipped** if the ammo slot is empty.
- In combat the ammo line counts down live. Below **Critical** you get a red warning in the middle of the screen.
- **Restock at vendors** (beta): open a vendor who sells your ammunition and the **Restock** window offers to fill up to **Refill to**. Click **Buy** – nothing is bought automatically.

::: tip Recommended setup
Set **Refill to** for ammunition to what fits your quiver plus one stack, and keep **Critical** at 50 so you are warned in time even during a fight.
:::

# Hunter

- **Class Buffs:** **Aspect** – with the **Variant menu on right-click** (beta) you pick Hawk, Monkey, Cheetah, Pack, Wild and more; left-click casts the last used one. Trueshot Aura (talent).
- **Pet** (beta):
  - **Pet missing:** left-click calls your pet; right-click offers Call Pet or Revive Pet.
  - **Pet dead:** left-click revives it.
  - **Feed pet:** when your pet is content or unhappy (**Only when unhappy** is an option). Left-click feeds the best food your pet accepts; right-click shows **Pet food in your bags** – only food your pet actually eats – with ★ favorites. No reminder while your pet is eating, in combat or while mounted. **No pet food** if nothing suitable is in your bags.
- **Supplies** (beta): **Ammunition** – counts the ammo you have equipped (default minimum 200, critical 50). **No ammo equipped** if the ammo slot is empty. Below critical during a fight you get a red warning. Optional: **Pet food** as a supply.

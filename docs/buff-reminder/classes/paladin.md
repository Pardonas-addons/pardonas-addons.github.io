# Paladin

- **Class Buffs:** **Aura**, **Seal**, **Blessing**; Righteous Fury (off by default). With the **Variant menu on right-click** (beta) you pick e.g. Devotion or Retribution aura, Seal of Righteousness or Wisdom, Blessing of Might or Kings; left-click casts the last used one.
- **Group** (beta): **Paladin blessings in group buffs** – your Greater Blessing on players who miss it. **Off while Pally Power is loaded.**
- **Supplies** (beta): Symbol of Kings for Greater Blessings, Symbol of Divinity

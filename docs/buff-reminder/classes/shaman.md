# Shaman

<ClassHeader cls="shaman" />

Shamans keep a shield on themselves and an imbue on each weapon. The Buff Reminder checks all three and recasts the one you use.

Features marked <Badge type="warning" text="beta" /> are experimental: on in beta versions, off by default in releases – see [Versions](../#versions).

## At a glance
| Reminder | Shows when | Left-click | Tab |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Shield** | no shield is active | casts the shield you used last | **Class Buffs** |
| **Main-hand weapon imbue** | your main-hand weapon has no imbue | casts the imbue you used last | **Class Buffs** |
| **Off-hand weapon imbue** | your off-hand weapon has no imbue | casts the imbue you used last | **Class Buffs** |
| Reagents <Badge type="warning" text="beta" /> | fewer than **Min.** in your bags | outside of towns: remind me in the next town; at a vendor: restock | **Supplies** |

## Class Buffs
- **Shield** from level 8: Lightning, Water or Earth Shield (Water and Earth only if the game has them).
- **Main-hand weapon imbue** from level 10, **Off-hand weapon imbue** from level 40 (only with a weapon in your off hand): Rockbiter, Flametongue, Frostbrand, Windfury, Earthliving.
- Left-click casts the variant you used last; with the **Variant menu on right-click** (beta) you pick another one.
- With the **Expiry warning** (beta) imbues are warned about before they run out (by time; imbues have no charges).

## Supplies <Badge type="warning" text="beta" />
| Reagent | For | From level | Min. | Refill to |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ankh | Reincarnation | 30 | 1 | 2 |
| Shiny Fish Scale | Water Breathing | 22 | 10 | 20 |
| Fish Oil | Water Walking | 28 | 10 | 20 |

::: tip Recommended setup
Enhancement shamans: cast your main-hand and off-hand imbue once each – the addon remembers both separately. Raise **Min.** for Ankh to 2 before a raid.
:::

# Shaman

- **Class Buffs:** **Shield** – Lightning, Water or Earth Shield; **Main-hand weapon imbue** and **Off-hand weapon imbue** – Rockbiter, Flametongue, Frostbrand, Windfury, Earthliving. With the **Variant menu on right-click** (beta) you pick which one; left-click casts the last used one.
- **Supplies** (beta): Ankh for Reincarnation, reagents for Water Walking and Water Breathing

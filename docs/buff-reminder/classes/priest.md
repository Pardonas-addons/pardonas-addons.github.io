# Priest

<ClassHeader cls="priest" />

Priests carry the most self-buffs and the most group buffs of all. The Buff Reminder keeps them all up, casts the prayers on your group and makes sure you never run out of candles.

Features marked <Badge type="warning" text="beta" /> are experimental: on in beta versions, off by default in releases – see [Versions](../#versions).

## At a glance
| Reminder | Shows when | Left-click | Tab |
|---|---|---|---|
| Power Word: Fortitude | you don't have it | casts it (highest rank) | **Class Buffs** |
| Inner Fire | you don't have it | casts it | **Class Buffs** |
| Divine Spirit | you don't have it (talent) | casts it | **Class Buffs** |
| Shadow Protection | you don't have it | casts it | **Class Buffs** |
| Vampiric Embrace, Shadowform | you don't have it (off by default) | casts it | **Class Buffs** |
| Prayers <Badge type="warning" text="beta" /> | party or raid members miss a buff | group version on one of them | **Group** |
| Candles, feathers | fewer than **Min.** in your bags | outside of towns: remind me in the next town; at a vendor: restock | **Supplies** |

## Class Buffs
- Power Word: Fortitude from level 1, Inner Fire from 12, Shadow Protection from 30. Divine Spirit is a talent.
- Vampiric Embrace and Shadowform are for Shadow priests and therefore off by default.
- Left-click casts the single-target version on yourself, never the prayer.

## Group <Badge type="warning" text="beta" />
Prayer of Fortitude, Prayer of Shadow Protection and Prayer of Spirit (Spirit only for players who use mana). A group line shows how many players miss the buff; left-click casts the prayer on one of them, **Ctrl+Left-click** the single version. **Check in**: **Always**, **Dungeons & raids only** or **Raids only**.

## Supplies
| Reagent | For | From level | Min. | Refill to |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Holy Candle | Prayer of Fortitude (rank 1) | 48 | 10 | 20 |
| Sacred Candle | higher ranks of the prayers | 56–60 | 10 | 20 |
| Light Feather | Levitate | 34 | 10 | 20 |

Only the candle your highest prayer rank needs is checked.

::: tip Recommended setup
Shadow priests: tick Shadowform and Vampiric Embrace. Healers: tick **Group buffs** with **Dungeons & raids only** and turn on the **Expiry warning** so you renew Fortitude before the raid notices.
:::

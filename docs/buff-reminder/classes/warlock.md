# Warlock

- **Class Buffs:** **Armor** – Demon Skin, Demon Armor or Fel Armor (pick with the **Variant menu on right-click**, beta)
- **Class consumables** (beta): healthstone and soulstone
- **Pet** (beta): **Demon missing** – left-click summons the demon set under **Demon to summon** (default **Last summoned**); right-click lets you pick any demon you know. Without a soul shard, left-click summons your Imp.
- **Supplies** (beta): soul shards (default minimum 5, critical 1)

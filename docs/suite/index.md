# The suite

Pardona's Addons are a small family of addons for WoW Forever that share one look and one way of working.

- **Every addon works on its own.** You can install any single addon; it brings everything it needs, including the shared Pardona library.
- **Installed together they act as one suite.** All Pardona addons share
  - one [minimap button](./minimap-button) with a menu for every installed addon,
  - one look (the Pardona style),
  - one settings group "Pardona's Addons", whose main page is an overview of all installed Pardona addons, with each installed addon as a sub-page (see [Settings](./settings)).
- **Each addon keeps its own settings.** Removing one addon does not affect the others.
